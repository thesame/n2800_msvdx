The demo is a conservatively re-encoded excerpt from the W3C-hosted trailer:
https://media.w3.org/2010/05/bunny/trailer.mp4

Changes: scaled to 1280×720, 25 fps, Main H.264, constrained reference/prediction
settings and VBV buffer to remain below the prototype slice limit; audio
downmixed to stereo AAC. This is a real movie excerpt, not a synthetic hardware
test. The launchers mute it by default. The bundled file's SHA256 is recorded
in provenance.json and MANIFEST.sha256.
